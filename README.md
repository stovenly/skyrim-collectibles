# Skyrim Collectibles Tracker

**<https://stovenly.github.io/skyrim-collectibles/>**

Drop a Skyrim save (`.ess`) on the page and get back what you still have to collect, as a list and as pins on maps
of Skyrim, the Forgotten Vale, Solstheim and the Soul Cairn:

- Dragon Priest Masks
- Stones of Barenziah
- Daedric Artifacts, with the game's own Oblivion Walker count
- Dragon Claws
- Black Books
- Words of Power
- Paragons
- Jiub's Opus
- Kagrumez Resonance Gems
- Insects in Jars
- Thieves Guild Trophies

Saves from Skyrim (2011), Skyrim Special Edition (including the Anniversary Edition) and Skyrim VR all work, with or
without Dawnguard and Dragonborn; collectibles from a DLC appear only when the save has it. Anything a quest has put
out of reach, like a mask left behind in Skuldafn or an artifact turned down, is marked as lost.

The three insect jars from Saints & Seducers, the free Creation Club content every Special Edition has had since the
Anniversary update, appear for saves that have it. Other Creation Club content adds nothing to these lists.

Assumes an otherwise unmodded game. Mods that move or remove collectibles will throw the results off.
