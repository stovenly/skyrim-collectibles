Find the Stones of Barenziah you are missing

The 24 Stones of Barenziah are scattered through palaces, tombs and bandit caves. Bring them all to Vex in the Ragged Flagon during No Stone Unturned and Barenziah's Crown goes back on its stand.

The quest only starts once you have found the first stone and joined the Thieves Guild, and the game counts them without ever telling you where the rest are.

What this page does

Drop your save in above, from Skyrim, Skyrim Special Edition or Skyrim VR, and it works out which of the Stones of Barenziah you already have. Whatever is left is listed with its location and pinned on the map.

The save is read in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and there is nothing to install.

The tracker checks itself against the game's own stone counter and warns you if they disagree.

If you would rather see everything at once, the full tracker covers every kind of collectible together.
